Ease everythingNothing snaps. Every frame the front wheels, the steering wheel and the camera move part of the way toward where they should be: now += (target − now) × dt × ease. From Bruno's VisualVehicle.js.
Body lean + nose dipThe wheels follow the road; the body sits on springs. It leans out of a lane change, squats when you floor it and dips when you brake, then wobbles back. The car's "real" position never changes; it's only for looks.
Brake lightsTail lights glow softly while driving and flare when you brake. Cheap, and it reads beautifully in black and white.
Speed cameraThe camera trails a smoothed point, leans into the lane you're moving to, and pulls back with a wider lens as you go faster. From Bruno's View.js.
Camera kickA quick roll that springs back: when you floor it, scrape the rail or hit a joint hard. In the game: gunshots, near misses, a story beat landing.
Engine + jointsEngine pitch and volume follow speed and throttle. Expansion joints every 40 m give a soft thump-thump, felt in the body and heard. Tap the stage once to allow sound.
Pull over: SelahHold the brake to a stop and you stay parked. A stop event fires (with a gap between the stop and start speeds so it never flickers), the camera drifts to the rail and looks across the bay. Press ↑, change lanes or tap to drive on.
Phone steeringDrag anywhere: a stick appears under your thumb. Left and right steer smoothly between lanes; push up to speed up (small pushes creep, a full push floors it), pull down to brake. Short taps still change lanes.
